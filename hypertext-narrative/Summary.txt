Station Lockdown is a short interactive horror story set in a police station overrun after a city-wide outbreak. The player begins in the main lobby, where the power is flickering, and chooses between taking the hallway or the stairs. Three of the four routes end in a deadly encounter with a zombie, a grotesque mutated creature, or an unstoppable tyrant. Only one route, the police chief's office, leads to a weapon and a note that gives the player a chance to survive. Every dead end offers a way back to the lobby so the player can try again.
